🌊 SeaSenseAI Overview

SeaSenseAI is an AI-powered solution designed to support fishermen by providing fish availability prediction, weather insights, market price estimation, and safety alerts.

🌊 Problem Statement

Small-scale fishermen face several challenges that directly affect their safety, productivity, and income. Unpredictable weather conditions, limited knowledge of fish availability, fluctuating market prices, and the lack of real-time information often lead to unsuccessful fishing trips, increased fuel costs, and financial losses.

Most fishermen rely on traditional experience and word-of-mouth rather than data-driven insights, making it difficult to plan efficient and safe fishing operations. In addition, there is no single platform that combines weather updates, fish location predictions, market price information, and emergency support.

As a result, fishermen spend more time and resources at sea while facing higher risks and uncertain earnings. There is a need for an intelligent, accessible, and user-friendly solution that helps fishermen make informed decisions and improve their livelihoods.
